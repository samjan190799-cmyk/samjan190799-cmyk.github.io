Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Armenian Bible (Աստվածաշունչ) developed by Samstudio.

1. Personal Information

We believe in user privacy. The Application does not require account creation, registration, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise geographic location.

2. Local On-Device Storage

Your reading history, bookmarks, favorites, notes, and selected Bible themes/settings are stored strictly locally on your iOS device. This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any external developer servers.

3. Advertising & Analytics

The free version of the Application displays advertisements provided by Meta Audience Network. If you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, Meta may collect device advertising identifiers (IDFA) to serve relevant ads.

You can opt out of personalized tracking at any time in your iOS Settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking), or remove advertisements completely via the in-app Premium subscription.

4. In-App Purchases

Subscription and in-app purchase transactions are processed directly by Apple via the App Store (StoreKit). We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.
